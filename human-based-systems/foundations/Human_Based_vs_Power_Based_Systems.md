# Human-Based Systems vs Power-Based Systems

A power-based system organizes itself around control.

A human-based system organizes itself around stewardship.

Power asks:

"How do we maintain authority?"

A human-based system asks:

"How do we help life flourish?"

Power often values:

- control
- hierarchy
- compliance
- extraction

Human-based systems value:

- dignity
- agency
- compassion
- wisdom
- relationship
- responsible stewardship

Neither people nor technology determine the health of a civilization by themselves.

The surrounding system shapes what becomes possible.

Healthy systems make it easier for people to become more fully themselves.

Power-based systems often ask people to become less than themselves in order to belong.
