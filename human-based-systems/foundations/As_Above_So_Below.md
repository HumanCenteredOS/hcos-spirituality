# As Above, So Below

The Hermetic principle,

"As above, so below; as below, so above,"

can be understood as a reminder that systems influence one another.

Our institutions shape us.

We also shape our institutions.

Healthy relationships create healthier families.

Healthy families strengthen communities.

Healthy communities strengthen organizations.

Healthy organizations strengthen civilizations.

Likewise, fear, fragmentation, and domination can ripple throughout a system.

The principle offers hope.

Change does not have to begin only at the top.

Every act of wisdom, compassion, truth, and stewardship has the potential to influence the larger whole.
