# Hermetic Principles and Systems

This document explores several Hermetic ideas through the lens of human systems.

The best known principle is:

> As above, so below.
> As below, so above.

Within this framework, systems continuously influence one another.

Individuals shape families.

Families shape communities.

Communities shape institutions.

Institutions shape civilizations.

The relationship is reciprocal.

Small acts of truth, compassion, and stewardship can influence larger systems over time.

Likewise, unhealthy systems can reinforce fear, domination, and fragmentation.

Whether interpreted spiritually or symbolically, the principle reminds us that meaningful change can begin at every level.
