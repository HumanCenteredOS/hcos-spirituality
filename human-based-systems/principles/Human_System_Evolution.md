# Human System Evolution

Civilizations evolve.

Technology evolves.

People evolve.

Healthy evolution requires more than increasing capability.

It requires increasing wisdom.

As intelligence becomes more widely available, the quality of the surrounding human system becomes increasingly important.

Healthy systems evolve toward:

- Greater dignity
- Greater stewardship
- Greater trust
- Greater transparency
- Greater compassion
- Greater agency

The movement from power-based systems toward human-based systems represents not abandoning structure, but remembering what structure exists to serve.

The future of civilization will be shaped not only by what becomes possible, but by the values that guide those possibilities.
