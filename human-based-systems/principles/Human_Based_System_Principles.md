# Human-Based System Principles

Healthy systems exist to help life flourish.

They recognize that people influence systems.

Systems also influence people.

Human-based systems strive to preserve:

- Human dignity
- Agency
- Truth
- Compassion
- Wisdom
- Presence
- Stewardship
- Relationship

Healthy systems ask:

"How can we help people become more fully themselves?"

rather than

"How can we make people easier to manage?"

Technology, policy, leadership, and culture all exist to support life—not replace it.
