# Destiny and Soul Continuity

Destiny is understood here as the unique pattern of relationships, gifts, responsibilities, and opportunities belonging to a soul.

Sometimes people experience a deep feeling that they are living someone else's life.

Within this framework, that experience is understood as fragmentation rather than failure.

Healing involves:

- Truth
- Consent
- Release
- Restoration

The goal is not becoming someone new.

The goal is becoming more fully who we have always been.
