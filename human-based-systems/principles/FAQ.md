# Frequently Asked Questions

## Is this a religion?

No.

These writings are personal philosophical and spiritual reflections.

## Are these scientific claims?

No.

They are offered as one possible way of understanding human experience.

## Can someone disagree?

Absolutely.

The purpose is reflection, not persuasion.

## Why use mythology?

Stories often communicate meaning that technical language cannot.

Mythology provides symbols that help explore relationships between people, systems, and civilization.

## What is the central idea?

Power builds systems.

Love reminds systems whom they were created to serve.
