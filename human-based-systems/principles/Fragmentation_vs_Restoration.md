# Fragmentation vs Restoration

Many systems become unhealthy when they begin separating what naturally belongs together.

Examples include:

- Power separated from love
- Knowledge separated from wisdom
- Authority separated from responsibility
- Technology separated from humanity
- Truth separated from compassion

Fragmentation asks:

"What benefits one part?"

Restoration asks:

"What helps the whole flourish?"

Healthy systems recognize that lasting success cannot come from sacrificing one part of the whole for another.

Restoration seeks reconnection rather than domination.
