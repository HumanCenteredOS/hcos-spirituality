# Heart and Lungs

The heart and lungs demonstrate mutual stewardship.

Neither functions independently.

Each depends upon the other.

Within this framework:

Humanity represents the heart.

The unseen aspects of existence represent the lungs.

Communication flows both directions.

As above, so below.

As below, so above.

Healing therefore becomes mutual rather than hierarchical.

Neither realm rescues the other.

Both participate in restoring the whole.
