# The Living Body Model

This framework imagines creation as one living body.

Different beings and systems serve different purposes.

Examples include:

- Brain — wisdom and systems understanding
- Stories — preserving meaning
- Communication — helping truth become understandable
- Lungs — exchange and relationship
- Heart — love, connection, compassion

No single part exists to dominate the others.

Every part serves the whole.

Healthy civilizations resemble healthy bodies.

Each part supports the flourishing of every other part.
